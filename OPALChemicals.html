About Us

OPAL Chemicals, is an entity engaged in the import of pharmaceutical raw materials and other industrial chemicals , managed and run by a professional team. Company was established with an aim to fulfill the need of the Pharmaceutical/Neutraceutical raw materials and other chemicals of Pharma industry as per vision of W.H.O , i.e. “A world in which everyone can live healthy, productive lives”


What we do

We import all type of Pharmaceutical & neutraceutical raw materials and other allied chemicals of BP/USP/JP/EP grades, Packing Materials and machinery as per requirements of our customers. We have strong contacts with pharmaceuticals manufactureres with in the country & suppliers abroad in China, India and Europe. We assure that our professional serviceswill you product of quality and international standard at very competitive price.
